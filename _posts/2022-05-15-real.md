title: Be real
excerpt: Not optimistic, not pessimistic. Real. Why that's the right mindset for anyone chasing the truth.

---

If you're a scientist, or anyone in pursuit of the truth, the mindset to hold is realism. Not optimistic, not pessimistic. Real.

Optimists think things will eventually get better on their own. They won't. Left alone, things tend to fall apart (cue entropy). Pessimists expect the worst and think nothing can be done about it. I don't believe that either.

Being real means seeing things as they are. That makes you curious: you want to know more about the subject and get to the truth. And when you find something, the reward pushes you to stay realistic.

It's easy to mix realism up with the other two.

Sometimes realism looks like optimism. That's because seeking the truth has produced a lot of good stuff so far. The iPhone, which beats no phone, traces back to Maxwell's theory of electromagnetism.

Sometimes realism looks like pessimism. To discover anything, you have to look hard at what isn't working right now. That's hard, often fruitless, and sometimes hopeless.

Know the difference and keep your balance. Go digging, and be real!
